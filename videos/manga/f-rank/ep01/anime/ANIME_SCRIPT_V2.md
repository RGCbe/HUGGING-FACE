# THE F-RANK TEA SELLER — Episode 1: "Hunter's Chai"
## 2D Anime Director's Script & Continuity Plan (V2)

> **Visual Style:** 2D Anime Cel-Shaded (*Solo Leveling* / *Dragon Ball Super* aesthetic — bold ink outlines, vibrant cel shading, radial speed lines, impact flashes).  
> **Rule #1:** Never adapt manga panels one-by-one. Every scene is written as continuous physical movement, camera choreography, and audio bridges (J-cuts / L-cuts).

---

## Cast & Locked Visual Anchors
* **ARJUN (18):** Messy black hair, warm brown skin, white collarless kurta, red waist sash, red string bracelet. Calm, observant, effortless reflexes.
* **VIKTOR HALE (28):** Tall, slicked-back blonde hair, polished silver plate armor, black cape with silver clasps, golden eyes when powered up. Loud B-rank livestreamer.
* **MEERA (22):** Single thick black braid, white healer robe with gold trim, carved wooden staff with green crystal.
* **RAGHU (35):** Huge, bald, bearded tank in heavy dark iron armor with a tower shield. Speaks with nods and gestures.

## ACT 2: THE STALL IN MUMBAI
**Location Lighting Lock:** *Mumbai Marine Drive & Gate Plaza, 6:30 PM golden-hour sunset dusk, warm amber lantern light against deep teal/blue Gate glow.*

### Scene 2 — Mumbai 2031 & The Tea Secret (0:15 – 0:38)
* **Audio (J-Cut):** City traffic, distant sirens, and crowd cheers fade in *before* the title card clears.
* **Shot 5 (2.5s — Pure T2V World):** High aerial swoop over Marine Drive's curved seafront at dusk. A colossal swirling blue Gate hovers over the Arabian Sea.
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"Mumbai, 2031. Gates to monster dungeons opened in every city on Earth."*
* **Shot 6 (2.5s — Pure T2V World):** Street-level tracking shot: armored hunters stride out of a plaza Gate carrying glowing blue monster cores while crowds cheer and film with phones.
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"Hunters go in. Monster cores come out. Hunters get rich."*
* **Shot 7 (2.0s — Match-on-Action):** Whip-pan down from the giant Gate to a tiny wooden tea stall tucked in its shadow. Brass pots bubble.
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"And right next to the biggest Gate in the city... stands a tiny tea stall."*
* **Shot 8 (2.0s — Macro Action):** Close-up on brown hands crushing green cardamom pods in a stone mortar. For 3 frames, a faint golden shimmer rises from the crushed seeds as he tips them into boiling milk tea.
* **Shot 9 (3.0s — Pure T2V Action):** Arjun lifts the steel kettle high, pouring a clean arc of glowing amber chai into a glass, then extends the glass toward the camera.
  * **ARJUN (LongCat):** *"Chai! Hot chai! Ten rupees a glass!"*
* **Shot 10 (2.5s — End-Frame Chained):** Hunters march past ignoring him; one kicks a paper cup across the stones. Arjun lowers the glass slightly with a dry, half-amused smile. Camera racks focus to his **F-Rank Hunter Card** hanging on a nail.
  * **ARJUN (LongCat):** *"Business is... a little slow today."*
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"Arjun. Eighteen. Rank F—the weakest rank there is."*

### Scene 3 — Viktor's Arrival & The Bet (0:38 – 1:15)
* **Audio (J-Cut):** Whirring camera drone + crowd chatter rises before the cut.
* **Shot 11 (2.5s — Pure T2V Crowd):** The plaza crowd parts. Viktor strides in with his silver armor and black cape flowing, smiling at a hovering livestream drone. Meera and Raghu flank him.
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"Then the real hunters arrived. Viktor Hale. B-Rank. Two million followers. Zero manners."*
* **Shot 12 (2.5s — LongCat Acting):** Viktor spots the stall, spins his floating drone toward Arjun, and points while laughing into the lens.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"Ha! Look, chat! It's the F-Rank tea boy!"*
* **Shot 13 (2.5s — Match-on-Action + LongCat):** Arjun doesn't flinch. He slides a fresh steaming glass across the counter with a calm smile.
  * **ARJUN:** *"Tea, sir? Good for the nerves before a dungeon."*
* **Shot 14 (2.5s — End-Frame Chained):** Viktor pinches the glass with two fingers, sniffs it dramatically for his stream, and smirks.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"Tea from an F-Rank? Is it poisoned with weakness?"*
* **Shot 15 (2.5s — LongCat Acting):** Meera steps into frame (180° axis preserved, facing Arjun left-to-right), dropping three silver coins into Arjun's palm with an apologetic smile.
  * **MEERA:** *"Viktor, be nice. Three teas, please."*
* **Shot 16 (2.0s — Match-on-Action):** Arjun pours two more glasses in one fluid sweep; Raghu's massive armored hand takes one and gives a single respectful nod.
* **Shot 17 (3.0s — Action Beat):** Viktor tilts his head back and downs the entire glass in one gulp for the drone. He wipes his mouth and tosses the empty glass backward over his shoulder without looking.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"...Hmph. Nothing special."*
* **Shot 18 (2.0s — Sakuga Reflex Reveal):** Seamless match-cut: the spinning glass flies toward the stall—Arjun catches it cleanly with two fingers *without looking up* from his pot.
  * **ARJUN (calm, quiet):** *"Good luck in there."*
* **Shot 19 (2.5s — End-Frame Chained):** Over Arjun's shoulder as the three hunters walk into the swirling blue Gate in the distance. Arjun's warm smile drops into razor-sharp focus, eyes reflecting the blue light.
  * **ARJUN (whispering):** *"One hour. That's all you get."*

## ACT 3: THE DUNGEON & SAKUGA BATTLE
**Location Lighting Lock:** *Dark Level 9 Cave, eerie blue mushroom glow, warm firelight from deeper chambers, high-contrast anime shadows.*

### Scene 4 — The Ambush (1:15 – 1:45)
* **Shot 20 (2.0s — Transition):** Viktor, Meera, and Raghu step through the swirling blue membrane into the damp cavern.
  * **VIKTOR (to drone):** *"Level 9. Easy content, chat."*
* **Shot 21 (1.5s — Pure T2V Monster Reveal):** Ceiling dust rains down as a massive 12-foot **Ogre** steps out of the shadows swinging a jagged cleaver, flanked by glowing-eyed shadow wolves.
* **Shot 22 (1.2s — Pure T2V Sakuga Impact):** The Ogre slams its cleaver down! Raghu braces his tower shield—**2-frame white/black impact flash**—sparks explode and the steel cracks as Raghu slides backward across the rock.
  * **RAGHU:** *"Ghhk...!"*
* **Shot 23 (1.5s — Pure T2V Sakuga):** Viktor lunges in for a counter-slash, gets clipped across the shoulder, and slams into a cave boulder.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"Tch! Since when are Level 9 ogres this strong?!"*
* **Shot 24 (2.0s — LongCat + VFX):** Meera raises her crystal staff; emerald healing light surges toward Raghu as she looks back in panic.
  * **MEERA:** *"Viktor! We have to retreat!"*

### Scene 5 — The One-Hour Awakening (1:45 – 2:25)
* **Audio (J-Cut):** Heartbeat (*BA-DUMP*) echoes over Meera's line.
* **Shot 25 (1.5s — Callback to Cold Open):** Golden veins of light surge up Viktor's arms. He stares at his trembling hands, eyes igniting gold.
* **Shot 26 (1.2s — Pure T2V Sakuga Burst):** **The Dash.** Viktor vanishes in a blur of golden lightning and radial speed lines, shattering the cave floor beneath his boots.
* **Shot 27 (1.0s — Pure T2V Sakuga Strike):** Diagonal golden energy slash tears across the screen—**2-frame inverted B&W impact frame**—the Ogre splits and dissolves into blue mana particles.
* **Shot 28 (2.0s — Reaction):** Meera and Raghu freeze, jaws dropped, hair blown back by the wind pressure.
  * **MEERA:** *"Did he just... one-shot it?!"*
* **Shot 29 (2.0s — LongCat Smirk):** Golden sparks drift around Viktor as he rests the greatsword on his shoulder and grins into his drone.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"Heh. Guess I just... awakened."*
* **Shot 30 (6.0s — Sakuga Speed Montage):** Four rapid **1.2s–1.5s Pure T2V combat bursts** chained with impact flashes: Viktor cutting through a pack of shadow wolves, leaping off stalagmites, golden trails lighting up the cavern, while a digital timer in the corner races `02:00 → 58:00`.
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"For the next fifty-eight minutes, Viktor was unstoppable."*

### Scene 6 — Minute Sixty (2:25 – 2:55)
* **Shot 31 (2.5s — Pure T2V Boss Reveal):** Massive obsidian doors blow open into a fiery throne room. A towering **Black-Armored Minotaur** rises from a stone throne, hoisting a colossal battleaxe.
* **Shot 32 (1.5s — Pure T2V Leap):** Viktor leaps high into the air, sword raised overhead, blazing gold.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"HYAAAH!! Boss time!"*
* **Shot 33 (1.5s — Timer Climax):** Corner clock hits `59:59 → 60:00` and flashes **RED**. Mid-air, Viktor's golden aura sputters out like a dying candle (*Fssshh!*).
  * **NARRATOR (V.O.):** *"Minute sixty."*
* **Shot 34 (1.8s — Comedic Crash):** Gravity hits. Viktor drops like a rock, landing face-first in front of the Minotaur's giant boots. His sword feels heavy again. He slowly looks up, sweating bullets.
  * **VIKTOR:** *"...Oh no."*
* **Shot 35 (2.0s — Pure T2V Chase):** Raghu sprints past, snatches Viktor by the back of his cape, and drags him backward as the Minotaur's axe smashes the floor behind them!
  * **VIKTOR:** *"RUN!!!"*
* **Shot 36 (1.8s — Escape):** All three sprint down the collapsing corridor with the Minotaur thundering behind them and dive headfirst into the exit Gate.
