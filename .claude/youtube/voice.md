# voice.md

Draft from the setup questions on 2026-10-20, updated the same day from two short phone voice
notes (a 15-second Tamil greeting and 33 seconds of reading English text aloud). Update "How I
actually talk" and "Words I do use" again from the transcript of the first real voiceover.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a Tamil-speaking working adult. Busy, watching on the phone in a tea break or on the
commute. Not a history expert and not a student. They want a fact or a story that makes them laugh
and that they can repeat to their friends at work the next day.

## How I actually talk

- Tanglish: Tamil mixed with English, in casual everyday speech.
- **Scripts are written in English letters**, e.g. "Idhu romba interesting fact". English words
  stay in English. Never Tamil script.
- Funny and casual, like telling a friend a story over tea. Short sentences. Jokes come from the
  story itself, not from forced punchlines.
- I'm Manoj. I greet viewers warmly as friends ("nanbargale") and ask how they and their family
  are, the way you'd greet someone you know.
- I read English text clearly and comfortably, so short English phrases and quotes are fine to
  keep in English.
- Do not invent catchphrases or slang for me beyond what is listed under "Words I do use".

## Words I never use

None given yet. Default: avoid stiff, formal or "AI-sounding" phrases ("dive in", "game-changer",
"unlock", "let's get into it").

## Words I do use

From my voice notes:

- "Vanakkam nanbargale"
- "Naan unga Manoj pesuren"
- "Eppadi irukeenga?" / "Veetla ellarum eppadi irukaanga?"

Keep the greeting short and put it AFTER the hook, never before it.

## What I will not claim

Defaults for a facts and history channel:

- No fact without a source I can name. If a fact is not certain, say so in the script.
- Legends, rumours and myths are called legends, rumours and myths, never presented as true history.
- No made-up numbers, dates or quotes.

## My format

- **Faceless.** Never on camera.
- **My own voice** for the voiceover, recorded on my phone.
- Topic: funny facts, history and true stories.
- Visuals: stock footage, old photos and maps, text on screen, simple animations.
- Thumbnails: no face. One bold image or object and at most three words.
- Length, upload days and Shorts plan: not decided yet.
