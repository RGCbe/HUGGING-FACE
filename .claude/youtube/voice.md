# voice.md

Draft from the setup questions on 2026-10-20, updated the same day from two short phone voice
notes (a 15-second Tamil greeting and 33 seconds of reading English text aloud). The channel is
**English only**. Update "How I actually talk" and "Words I do use" again from the first real
voiceover.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a busy working adult who watches in English, on the phone in a tea break or on the
commute. Not a history expert and not a student. They want a fact or a story that makes them laugh
and that they can repeat to their friends at work the next day.

## How I actually talk

- **Videos are in simple, clear English only.** No Tamil, no Tanglish.
- Funny and casual, like telling a friend a story over tea. Short sentences, everyday words. Jokes
  come from the story itself, not from forced punchlines.
- I'm Manoj. I greet viewers warmly, as friends.
- I read English text clearly and comfortably.
- Do not invent catchphrases or slang for me beyond what is listed under "Words I do use".

## Words I never use

None given yet. Default: avoid stiff, formal or "AI-sounding" phrases ("dive in", "game-changer",
"unlock", "let's get into it").

## Words I do use

- "Hello friends, I'm Manoj."

Keep the greeting short and put it AFTER the hook, never before it.

## What I will not claim

Defaults for a facts and history channel:

- No fact without a source I can name. If a fact is not certain, say so in the script.
- Legends, rumours and myths are called legends, rumours and myths, never presented as true history.
- No made-up numbers, dates or quotes.

## My format

- **Faceless.** Never on camera.
- **Voice:** my own voice, either recorded on my phone or an AI copy of it made from my own voice
  notes. When an AI copy is used, tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box at upload.
- Topic: funny facts, history and true stories.
- Visuals: stock footage, old photos and maps, text on screen, simple animations.
- Thumbnails: no face. One bold image or object and at most three words.
- Length, upload days and Shorts plan: not decided yet.
