In November 1932, wheat farmers in Western Australia, many of them former soldiers, asked for
help against around 20,000 emus. Major Meredith arrived with two soldiers, two Lewis guns and
10,000 rounds. Round one ended with a jammed gun and a retreat. Round two went on until
10 December, at about ten bullets for every emu. The farmers asked for the army again in 1934,
1943 and 1948 and were refused every time.
