In twenty thirty-one, gates to monster dungeons opened in every city on Earth.
And right next to the biggest gate in Mumbai, there was a tiny tea stall.
This is Arjun. Eighteen years old. F-Rank, the weakest rank there is.
The top hunter, Viktor, looked at his tea and laughed. Tea, from an F-Rank? Is it poisoned with weakness?
Arjun just smiled. Enjoy your tea, sir. Good luck in there.
One hour later, deep in the dungeon, Viktor felt a power he had never felt in his life.
Nobody knew the truth about Arjun's tea. One hour. That's all you get.
