# Manoj Explains

**Manoj Explains** is a YouTube channel (@manoj-d-explains) that explains the stories behind movies and series,
starting with Marvel lore, in English and in Tamil. Its narrator is a 3D anime version of its creator, Manoj.

## The "Manoj Explains uploader" app

The channel's owner uses a small private app, "Manoj Explains uploader", to upload his own videos to his own channel:
it uploads finished videos with their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and schedules, and adds them to the channel's
playlists. It is not offered to anyone else and has no other users.
