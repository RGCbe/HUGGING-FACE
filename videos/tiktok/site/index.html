☕ Tea Break Uploader

A private publishing tool for the creator Manoj (Tea Break with Manoj and Tea Break Comics).

Manoj makes short, funny true-history videos for the YouTube channel Tea Break with Manoj, and video reviews of his own webtoon, The F-Rank Tea Seller. Tea Break Uploader posts the vertical versions of those same videos to his own TikTok account, so he doesn't have to upload each one by hand.

What it does

  • Manoj connects his TikTok account once with TikTok's own login page and approves the permissions.
  • The tool uploads his finished videos to his TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API, with his caption.
  • It checks whether each upload finished.

It does not read anyone's messages, followers or other accounts, and it is not offered to other users.

See the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. Questions: the contact details on the About page of the YouTube channel Tea Break with Manoj.
