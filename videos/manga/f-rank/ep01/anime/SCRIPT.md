# THE F-RANK TEA SELLER
## Episode 1: "Hunter's Chai" (screenplay for the movie version)

Draft 1, 18 Nov 2026. Length: about 3.5 to 4 minutes (56 shots: 48 made by AI, 8 made in the edit; the shot list is `shots.json`). Look A (bright blockbuster; the dungeon darker). English; Tamil later.

How to read this: **SCENE** headings say where and when. Plain text is what we see. Names in CAPITALS speak.
(V.O.) = voice over (the narrator, Manoj). *Italic lines in brackets* explain to us why a moment is there.
`[p13]` = the webtoon panel the moment comes from. `[NEW]` = a new moment that connects the panels.

### Why the webtoon needs connecting
The webtoon jumps between pictures: tea stall, then dungeon, then the stall again. A film has to show how one moment
leads to the next. So the script adds small bridges: we see Arjun put the secret spice in the tea, we see Viktor
stream his fight to his fans, we see the clock run out, we see them run back out of the Gate and walk to the stall.
Every bridge is a cause-and-effect: **the tea is the cause of everything that happens in the dungeon.**

### The characters
- **ARJUN** (18): runs a tiny chai stall by the biggest Gate in Mumbai. A registered hunter, rank F, the lowest.
  Calm, polite, quietly funny. Knows more than he says.
- **VIKTOR HALE** (28): B-rank hunter and live-streamer with two million followers. Loud, vain, not evil.
- **MEERA** (22): healer in Viktor's party. Kind, honest, the only nice one.
- **RAGHU** (35): the party's tank. Huge, bald, bearded, says almost nothing; a gentle giant.
- **NARRATOR** (V.O.): Manoj.

## ACT ONE: THE TEA STALL

### SCENE 2. MUMBAI, MARINE DRIVE. DUSK.
`[p03]` + `[NEW]`
High above the curved seafront. Traffic lights shine like a necklace along the sea. In the sky over the bay hangs
a giant ring of blue light, slowly turning: a Gate.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Mumbai, 2031. Gates to monster dungeons have opened in every city on Earth.

`[NEW]` Down on the street: hunters in armor walk out of a smaller Gate carrying glowing blue crystals the size of
footballs. People cheer and film them on phones.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Hunters go in. Monster cores come out. Hunters get rich.

*[This tells a new viewer the rules of the world in ten seconds.]*

### SCENE 3. GATE PLAZA. EVENING.
`[p04]`
A huge stone square around the city's biggest Gate: a tall stone arch filled with swirling blue light. A queue of
hunters, police barriers, food carts, marigold strings, oil lamps.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): And right next to the biggest Gate in the city... stands a tiny tea stall.

The camera floats down to a small wooden stall with brass pots at the edge of the square.

### SCENE 4. ARJUN'S TEA STALL. CONTINUOUS.
`[NEW]` CLOSE: brown hands crush green cardamom pods in a small stone mortar. For one frame, a faint golden
shimmer rises from the crushed seeds, then it is gone. He tips them into the boiling milk tea.
*[We plant the secret here. Most viewers will miss it the first time.]*

`[p05]` ARJUN lifts the steel kettle high and pours a long stream of chai into a glass, without spilling a drop.

> ARJUN: Chai! Hot chai! Ten rupees a glass!

`[NEW]` Hunters walk past. Nobody stops. One kicks a paper cup past his stall.

`[p06]` Arjun looks at the full glass in his hand. A small, embarrassed smile.

> ARJUN: Business is... a little slow today.

`[NEW]` On a nail on the stall hangs his hunter licence card. Freeze frame. A clean card appears on screen:
**ARJUN / 18 / HUNTER RANK: F**

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Arjun. Eighteen years old. Hunter rank: F, the weakest rank there is.

*[The card explains why the hunters laugh at him later.]*

### SCENE 5. GATE PLAZA. MOMENTS LATER.
`[p07]` A wave of phones go up. The crowd parts. Three hunters walk through in slow motion:
VIKTOR in silver armor and black cape, MEERA in a white robe with a glowing staff, RAGHU with a tower shield.
`[NEW]` A small camera drone flies beside Viktor, filming him. He smiles into it.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Then the real hunters arrived.

Freeze on Viktor. A clean card: **VIKTOR HALE / B-RANK / 2M FOLLOWERS**

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Viktor Hale. B-Rank. Two million followers. Zero manners.

`[p08]` Viktor sees the stall. He turns the drone toward Arjun and points, laughing.

> VIKTOR: Ha! Look, everyone! It's the F-Rank tea boy!

The crowd laughs. Phones turn to Arjun.

`[p09]` Arjun does not react to the insult. He holds out a glass with a calm smile.

> ARJUN: Tea, sir? Good for the nerves before a dungeon.

`[p10]` Viktor takes it with two fingers, sniffs it, pulls a face for the camera.

> VIKTOR: Tea from an F-Rank? Is it poisoned with weakness?

`[p11]` MEERA steps up and holds out coins on her open palm, smiling an apology.

> MEERA: Viktor, be nice. Three teas, please.

Freeze on Meera. A clean card: **MEERA / HEALER**

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Meera. Healer. The only nice one in the party.

`[NEW]` Arjun pours two more glasses. RAGHU takes his with a huge hand and gives a small, polite nod.
Arjun nods back. *[Raghu is the quiet one. One nod is all he needs.]*

`[p12]` For the drone, Viktor drinks the whole glass in one gulp. GULP. He wipes his mouth.

> VIKTOR: ...Hmph. Nothing special.

`[NEW]` He tosses the empty glass back over his shoulder without looking. Arjun catches it without looking either.
*[A tiny hint that Arjun is not as weak as his card says.]*

> ARJUN: Good luck in there.

`[p13]` The party walks away toward the Gate. CLOSE on Arjun, lit by his lamp. He watches them go. The smile stays,
but his eyes are serious. Quietly, to himself:

> ARJUN: One hour. That's all you get.

## ACT TWO: THE DUNGEON

### SCENE 6. THE GATE. CONTINUOUS.
`[p14]` From behind: the three hunters walk into the wall of blue light. It swallows them.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Dungeon Gate. Level 9.

### SCENE 7. INSIDE THE GATE, LEVEL 9 CAVES.
`[NEW]` A huge cave lit by glowing blue mushrooms. The drone floats beside Viktor.

> VIKTOR (to the drone): Level 9. Easy content, chat.

`[p15]` A roar shakes dust from the ceiling. An OGRE three times their height steps out with a butcher's cleaver.
Wolves with red eyes circle behind it.

`[p16]` The ogre swings. RAGHU takes the blow on his shield. KRASH. The shield cracks and he flies backwards.

> RAGHU: Ghhk...!

`[p17]` VIKTOR slashes at the ogre and is knocked into the rock. A cut on his arm. He grits his teeth.

> VIKTOR: Tch! Since when are Level 9 ogres this strong?!

`[p18]` MEERA lifts her staff; green healing light pours over Raghu. She is scared.

> MEERA: Viktor! We have to retreat!

`[p19]` BA-DUMP. Viktor freezes. Golden light climbs his arms. He looks at his own hands.

> VIKTOR (thinking): What... is this power?!

`[p01]` His eyes turn yellow and the light bursts around him: the same shot as the cold open, no words this time.
*[Now the audience knows where the opening came from.]*

`[p20]` He moves faster than the eye can follow. One golden slash. KRAAACK. White flash. The ogre falls in two.

`[p21]` MEERA and RAGHU stare, mouths open.

> MEERA: Did he just... one-shot it?!

`[p22]` Viktor rests his sword on his shoulder, golden sparks falling around him. He grins at the drone.

> VIKTOR: Heh. Guess I just... awakened.

### SCENE 8. LEVEL 9, DEEPER. MONTAGE.
`[p23]` Fast cuts: Viktor cuts through a pack of wolves; golden trails in the dark; Meera and Raghu running to keep
up. A clean clock in the corner of the screen races from 02:00 to 58:00.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): For the next fifty-eight minutes, Viktor was unstoppable.

### SCENE 9. THE BOSS ROOM.
`[p24]` Huge doors open on a throne room of fire. A MINOTAUR in black armor rises from a stone throne, a giant axe
in its hands, red eyes burning.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Then they reached the boss.

`[NEW]`
> VIKTOR (to the drone): Boss time.

`[p25]` He leaps high, sword raised.

> VIKTOR: HYAAAH!!

The clock: 59:58... 59:59... **60:00**. It turns red.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Minute sixty.

`[p26]` In mid-air the golden light flickers... and goes out. Fsshh. Viktor lands badly in front of the minotaur.
His sword feels heavy again. He looks up at the monster. Pale, sweating.

> VIKTOR: ...Oh no.

`[p27]` `[NEW]` Raghu grabs Viktor by the cape and pulls.

> VIKTOR: RUN!!!

The three sprint down a corridor; the minotaur thunders after them. DOOM. DOOM. They dive into a wall of blue light.

## ACT THREE: THE TRUTH

### SCENE 10. GATE PLAZA. NIGHT.
`[p28]` The three tumble out of the Gate onto the stone. Viktor lies on his back, cape torn, breathing hard.
`[NEW]` Phones point at him from the crowd. The drone hovers above his face, still streaming.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): Outside the Gate. Barely alive.

`[NEW]` Viktor looks across the square. The little tea stall is still there, its lamp still burning. He gets up.

### SCENE 11. ARJUN'S TEA STALL. CONTINUOUS.
`[p29]` Viktor limps up and slams both hands on the counter. The glasses jump.

> VIKTOR: YOU! What did you put in that tea?!

`[p30]` Arjun looks at him. A long, calm beat. Steam drifts between them.

> ARJUN: ...Cardamom.

`[p31]` MEERA appears beside Viktor, coins already out, eyes shining.

> MEERA: Can I get one to go?

`[NEW]` Behind her, RAGHU silently holds up two fingers. *[The last laugh of the scene.]*

### SCENE 12. ARJUN'S TEA STALL. LATER THAT NIGHT.
`[p32]` The square is empty. One oil lamp. Arjun wipes glasses alone.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): That night. Nobody knew the truth about his tea.

`[p33]` A soft chime. A blue window of light opens in the air in front of him; only he can see it.

**[ HIDDEN SKILL ]**
**Name: Hunter's Chai**
**Rank: ???**
**Effect: Anyone who drinks it borrows great strength.**
**Duration: exactly 1 hour.**

Arjun reads it and smiles to himself.

> ARJUN (thinking): Ten rupees for an hour of power... I'm undercharging.

### SCENE 13. THE GATE. NIGHT.
`[p34]` The camera rises from the tiny stall up to the giant Gate. Deep inside the blue light, something moves.
A huge red eye opens, its pupil narrowing... looking straight down at the little tea stall.

> NARRATOR (V.O.): And deep inside the Gate... something had noticed.

## What changed from the webtoon (and why)
| New moment | Why it is there |
|---|---|
| Hunters carrying glowing cores out of a Gate | shows the world's rules instead of only saying them |
| The cardamom shimmer while Arjun makes the tea | plants the secret, so the "...Cardamom." ending pays off |
| His licence card on a nail | explains "F-Rank" before Viktor mocks it |
| Viktor's camera drone and "chat" lines | explains why he shows off, and why the crowd films his failure |
| Raghu's nod and two fingers | gives the quiet tank a personality with no extra dialogue |
| Arjun catches the glass without looking | a first hint that he is more than rank F |
| The cold open shot repeats in the cave | joins the opening to the story |
| The clock on screen | makes "one hour" visible, so minute sixty lands as a joke |
| Raghu pulling Viktor away; landing outside; walking to the stall | connects the dungeon back to the stall |
| Arjun's thought "I'm undercharging" | the webtoon's last joke, kept for the end |

All webtoon lines are kept word for word. New lines: "Level 9. Easy content, chat.", "Boss time." (Viktor),
"Ghhk...!" (Raghu, already in the webtoon), nothing else new is spoken.
