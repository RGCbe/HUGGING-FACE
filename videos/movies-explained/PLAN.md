# Movies and series explained (Manoj, 20 Nov 2026: "explained + theories", Tamil and English)

Hollywood movies and series, starting with Marvel, explained in our own words, plus theories, timelines and
watch order. Every video in English and in Tamil.

## The rule that shapes everything: no film footage

No clips, trailers, posters, stills, actors' photos, logos or film music. Studios claim them (Content ID): the
money goes to them or the video gets a strike. Instead:
- **Pictures:** our own illustrations in a bold comic style: places, objects (a glowing stone, a shield-shaped
  hint never the real design), silhouettes, timelines, maps, text cards. Never an actor's face, never a costume
  copied exactly.
- **Words:** the story retold in our own words with commentary (what it means, why it matters, what comes next).
- **Music:** our own.
- Film and character names in titles are fine (they describe what the video is about).

## Honesty

- Facts from official pages (Marvel, Disney), Wikipedia plot summaries and the trade press (Variety, The
  Hollywood Reporter). Each video lists its sources.
- A theory is always called a theory ("my theory", "fans think"), never news.
- Spoilers: the title says "spoilers" or "no spoilers"; an ending-explained waits until the full plot is published.

## Formats

| Format | Length | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **Explained:** one movie or series season, the whole story and what it means | 8-12 min | weekly |
| **Theories and explainers:** "Who is Doctor Doom?", "Every Avenger before Doomsday" | 5-8 min | weekly |
| **Shorts:** one theory, fact or "did you notice?" each | under 60 s | daily (English and Tamil) |
| **Watch order and timelines** | 5-10 min | now and then (people search for these all year) |

1. **What happened before Avengers: Doomsday:** the MCU's Multiverse Saga so far, in about 12 minutes.

3. **The Fantastic Four: First Steps and Thunderbolts\* explained** (both lead into Doomsday).
4. **Avengers: Doomsday, everything to know before you watch (no spoilers).** Theory Shorts every day that week.

Then: Spider-Man: Brand New Day explained; the Infinity Saga in order, one film a week (from Iron Man, 2008);
