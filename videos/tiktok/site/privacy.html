Privacy Policy

Tea Break Uploader · Last updated 6 November 2026

Tea Break Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that Manoj uses to post his own videos to his own TikTok account. This page explains what it does with data from TikTok.

1. Data the app receives from TikTok

Only after the account owner signs in with TikTok and approves it, the app receives:

  • basic account information (the account's open ID, display name and profile picture), to confirm which account is connected;
  • an access token and a refresh token, which let the app upload videos to that account;
  • the status of each upload (for example, whether it finished).

The app does not receive or read messages, followers, likes, contacts, other users' data, or the account's password.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok account and check that each upload worked. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Where it is kept

The tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private cloud environment. They are never put in public code or shared. Upload records are kept only as long as they are useful to the owner.

4. Sharing

Data from TikTok is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. This website has no ads, cookies or trackers.

5. Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, then Security & permissions, then Apps and services). The stored tokens then stop working and are deleted.

6. Children

The app is not meant for, or used by, anyone under 18.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: the contact details on the About page of the YouTube channel Tea Break with Manoj.
