# Movies and series: plan (Manoj's choices, 20 Nov 2026)

- **Hollywood-style movies, our own stories:** action, disaster, sci-fi and superhero films with a live-action
  look. Never a remake of a real film, never its characters, actors or names; every face is invented.
- **English and Tamil:** the English film is made first, with lip-sync; the Tamil version is a dub (the same
  picture, Tamil voices, like a Tamil-dubbed Hollywood film), so the GPU time is spent once.
- **Made in chapters:** each film is shot as 5-6 minute chapters, about one a week on the free PRO GPU time (40
  minutes a day; a chapter is about 50 shots, about two days of GPU). After the last chapter, the chapters are
  joined into the full film and uploaded again as one movie, in English and in Tamil.
- **Stories that suit free AI video:** a few characters, big visuals (weather, water, vehicles, cities, fire),
  close-up dialogue, short action moments. Long fights with many moves still look weak, so they stay quick.
- **Where:** the new webtoon channel becomes the movies and series channel (F-Rank Tea Seller and these films).
  F-Rank Episode 1 is the first test: its views and watch time decide how far we go.
- **Rules:** AI-content label ticked, original music, no gore (danger shown before or after, never wounds),
  children in danger are always saved.
- **Order:** finish the F-Rank Episode 1 movie, then Zero Hour chapter by chapter. The GPU time is shared, so the
  daily run does one project at a time.

## How a chapter is made (the same tools as F-Rank Episode 1)

script and shot list -> start pictures with the same cast (keyframes) -> motion clips (Wan, own Space) ->
talking shots with lip-sync (LongCat, own Space) -> voices (English, then Tamil) -> score and sound effects ->
edit (edit.py) -> every shot checked -> upload private for Manoj.

## Film 1: Zero Hour (Manoj: "no preference", chosen for what free AI video does well)

**Logline:** A cyclone turns towards the city on the day its new twelve-kilometre sea bridge opens. When a span
cracks with two thousand people trapped in traffic, the engineer who designed it has ninety minutes to bring them
home, and her own son is on a school bus in the middle of the bridge.

**Setting:** Coral Bay, an invented coastal city.

**Cast (invented faces):**
- **Maya Raghavan**, 34, the bridge's chief engineer: calm, exact, stubborn.
- **Arun**, 10, her son, on the school bus.
- **Captain Leo Brandt**, 40s, coast guard rescue pilot: tired, brave, funny under pressure.
- **Victor Hale**, 50s, the contractor: charming, rich; he used cheaper steel and wants it hidden.
- **Nila**, 20s, control-room operator: Maya's eyes on the sensors.

**Chapters:**
1. **The Crack.** Opening day, flags and drones; storm warnings brushed aside by Hale. Maya sees a sensor that
   should not move. Arun's bus rolls onto the bridge. The cyclone turns. A crack runs across the road; a span
   sags; traffic stops. Two thousand people are trapped.
2. **Ninety Minutes.** The control room: Maya works out that the cracked span will fall when the storm surge
   peaks, in ninety minutes. The wind grounds the helicopters. Hale tries to take over and blame the storm.
3. **The Bus.** Maya drives out onto the bridge against the wind. The school bus is stuck at the edge of the
   cracked span; she and the passengers get every child off, one by one.
4. **The Eye.** The eye of the cyclone passes over: twenty quiet minutes. Leo flies in through the calm and starts
   lifting people off. Nila finds Hale's real steel order; Maya confronts him on the radio.
5. **Zero Hour.** The storm comes back. The last cars, the last people, and Maya's plan to save the bridge's
   other spans. The span falls into an empty sea. Morning: Arun asleep in Leo's helicopter.
