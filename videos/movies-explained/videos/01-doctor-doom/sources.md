# Who is Doctor Doom?: sources (read 20 Nov 2026)

- Wikipedia, "Doctor Doom": created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby; first appeared in The Fantastic Four #5 (cover-dated
  July 1962); Victor von Doom, monarch of the fictional European country Latveria; "a master of both science and
  sorcery"; early issues: "learned magic in Tibet"; metal mask over a scarred face, magically forged armour;
  "diplomatic immunity as head of state shields him from prosecution for crimes committed outside Latveria";
  Reed Richards is "his old college rival", whom he blames for his disfigurement in a laboratory accident; Kirby
  gave him "only a tiny scar on his cheek", John Byrne made his face "truly ravaged"; Secret Wars (2015): "he
  claims godhood over a reconstructed multiverse before Mister Fantastic defeats and temporarily redeems him".
- Wikipedia, "Avengers: Doomsday"; Marvel.com: in US cinemas 18 December 2026; directed by Anthony and Joe Russo;
  Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom (announced July 2024); the Avengers, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men converge to
  face Doom; Avengers: Secret Wars, 17 December 2027.
- Commentary in the script (not facts): "most villains have one trick, Doom has three", the Iron Man / Doctor Strange
  comparison, the closing question.
