# Manoj, the channel's narrator (3D anime, future tech)

A 3D anime version of Manoj himself (made from his own photo at his request, 20 Nov 2026; the photo is kept
private, never in this repository), with one face (`kavi_face.png`) and four outfits, one for each kind of video. Picture model: FLUX.2 [klein] (Apache-2.0).
The channel is Manoj Explains. New pictures of him are made from `kavi_face.png` plus the outfit picture as references, so he always looks the same.

| Outfit | Picture | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Black techwear jacket with glowing teal lines, teal visor, floating golden screens, brass tea charm | `kavi_1.jpg` | the daily 2-3 minute lore questions |
| 2. Teal high-collar sci-fi coat with orange light strips, with his floating robot sidekick | `kavi_2.jpg` | theory videos ("theory time"): the robot reacts |
| 3. White and teal tech jacket with a wristband projecting a holographic ring screen | `kavi_3.jpg` | news, release dates, watch order and timelines |
| 4. Long grey coat with glowing circuit lines, orange scarf, crystal cube projecting planets | `kavi_4.jpg` | cosmic and deep lore (Infinity Stones, the multiverse) and the long compilations |

He appears at the start (the question) and the end (the answer and the comments line) of each video; the
middle shows the story's own scenes.
