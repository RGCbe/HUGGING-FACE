# How Manoj talks: script rules for every video

The voice is the show. A script is Manoj telling a friend a story over tea, not an article read
aloud. These rules are for every script: Tea Break history videos, manga recaps, Shorts, TikToks.

## The test

Say each line out loud. If you wouldn't say it that way to a friend at a tea stall, rewrite it.

## Ten rules

1. **Contractions, always:** it's, he's, don't, can't, that's, they'd, wasn't.
   "It is the year twenty thirty-one." becomes "So it's twenty thirty-one."
2. **Mix the rhythm.** A longer line that carries the story, then a short punch. Never three short
   sentences of the same shape in a row ("This is Arjun. He is eighteen. Business is slow.").
3. **Link the facts the way people talk:** so, and then, but here's the thing, turns out, which
   means, now, because, and of course. A story flows from one thing to the next; it doesn't start
   over with a new subject in every sentence.
4. **React like a person.** About one honest reaction or small opinion a minute: "Honestly? I'd
   have run too." "Bold move." "This is my favourite part." Manoj's take is what makes the video
   his, and it is the human input YouTube looks for when it decides what can earn.
5. **Talk to the viewer now and then:** "Imagine you're the general...", "You can guess what
   happened next.", a real question at a turning point, then a pause.
6. **Tell the story, don't describe the picture.** The picture already shows Viktor laughing. The
   voice says why it matters or how it feels: not "Viktor points at Arjun and laughs." but "And of
   course Viktor has to make it a show."
7. **Concrete beats general:** the real number, the real object, the exact words someone said.
8. **Jokes:** set it up straight, put the funny word last, then stop (the music drops there).
   Never explain a joke. Call back to an earlier joke near the end.
9. **A turn every 30 to 40 seconds:** a new question, a reversal, a surprise, a small cliffhanger.
10. **Fresh words every video.** The only fixed sentence is the greeting, "Hello friends, I'm
    Manoj." Nothing else is copied from video to video: no "In this video, I'll tell you...",
    no stock ending. End on a take or a question about this story.

## Never

"dive in", "game-changer", "unlock", "let's get into it", "little did they know", "but then, he
discovers a shocking secret" (name the secret instead), "in conclusion", "it is the year", "here
is", chains like "This is X. He is Y.", or reading a speech bubble word for word and then
describing the same picture.

## Dialogue in manga recaps

Quote one or two lines that matter per scene, with attitude and who said it ("and Viktor, being
Viktor, goes: tea from an F-Rank? Is it poisoned with weakness?"). Say the rest in your own words.
The bubbles are on screen; the voice adds the attitude, not a second reading.

## Writing for the AI voice without sounding like a robot

- Punctuation is direction: "..." is a pause before a reveal, a dash is a sudden turn, a question
  mark is a real question.
- One idea per sentence carries the stress; put that word last ("...and the one who walked in was
  his father.").
- Numbers are written as they are said ("twenty thirty-one", "ten rupees").
- The voice can swallow a tiny first word ("He", "A", "And") at the start of a sentence that
  begins a new piece. Join that sentence to the one before with a comma or "and", or start with
  the name.
- If the listening test fails a line, rephrase it the way a person would say it. Don't chop it
  into shorter sentences; that is what makes a script sound like a computer.
- Emotion tags still start the line (`[excited]`, `[funny]`, ...).

## Before and after

> **Before:** It is the year twenty thirty-one. Gates to monster dungeons have opened in every
> city on Earth. Hunters go in, and they come out rich.
>
> **After:** So it's twenty thirty-one, and there are monster Gates in every city on Earth.
> Hunters walk in, fight whatever's inside, and walk out rich. Well... the ones who walk out.

> **Before:** This is Arjun. He is eighteen years old, and his hunter rank is F, the lowest rank
> there is. Business is slow.
>
> **After:** And this is Arjun. Eighteen, rank F, which is the lowest rank they've got, so
> instead of fighting monsters, he sells them tea. Business? Not great.

> **Before:** Hello friends, I'm Manoj. In this video, I'll tell you how one pig started the Pig
> War, what happened next, and who won in the end. Every word is true, and the sources are in the
> description.
>
> **After:** Hello friends, I'm Manoj. Grab your tea, because this one has a farmer, a very hungry
> pig, and America and the British Empire getting ready for war. Over one pig. And yes, it really
> happened.

## Check before voicing

- Contractions everywhere they would be said.
- No three same-shape short sentences in a row.
- One reaction or opinion a minute, in Manoj's voice.
- No line that only describes the picture.
- Nothing copied from an earlier video except the greeting.
